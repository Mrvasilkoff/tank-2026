TANK 2026

CONTROLS:

  • Keys can be changed in SETTINGS (also difficulty, your name, special CONQUEST enemies and night rules)
  • Gamepad: d-pad / stick — move, A B X Y RB RT — fire, LB — headlights, START — pause
  • 3-4 PLAYERS: I and II on the keyboard, III and IV on gamepads, big map with two bases
  • 1 player: WASD / arrows — move, SPACE / J / click — fire
  • 2 players — I: WASD — move, SPACE / F — fire
  • 2 players — II: arrows — move, ENTER / NUM0 / RIGHT CTRL — fire
  • ONLINE: one player picks CREATE GAME and a mode, then sends COPY LINK (or the room code). Friends open the link or pick JOIN GAME and type the code. Everyone waits in the lobby until the host presses START. Your room code stays the same between games, so a saved link keeps working
  • CHAT (online): T — type a message, ENTER — send, ESC — cancel. Quick phrases: 1 HELP · 2 COVER THE BASE · 3 FOLLOW ME · 4 GG (gamepad: hold SELECT and press ← ↓ ↑ →)
  • CONQUEST: an endless march north with no base of your own. Enemies come towards you and cities stand in the way — destroy the red HQ and the land becomes yours. The flag on the panel counts captured cities
  • Night in CONQUEST: Q (player II: «.», gamepad: LB) switches your headlights off: enemies only notice you up close, but a shot gives you away. City searchlights raise the alarm (the lamp on the HQ can be shot out). At night there are more fast enemies, artillery misses, water freezes, kills score ×1.5 and the flare bonus lights everything around. Every rule can be switched in SETTINGS → NIGHT
  • BIG MAP 2-4: wide map with two bases for 2–4 players. Losing either base is GAME OVER
  • P / ESC — pause menu, M — sound, N — new map

TERRAIN:

  • BRICK: Destroyed by standard tank shells.
  • STEEL: Indestructible (until Tier 4 Star Upgrade!).
  • WATER: Blocks tanks, but bullets can fly across! (Equip Boat to cross).
  • TREES: Tanks and bullets pass through; can hide tanks from view!
  • ICE: Slippery surface with inertia and drifting.

WEAPON UPGRADES:

  • ⭐ 0 Stars: Basic cannon, single shell.
  • ⭐ 1 Star: High-velocity shell.
  • ⭐⭐ 2 Stars: Twin-barrel cannons, 2 shells.
  • ⭐⭐⭐ 3 Stars: Destroys STEEL walls and trees.

ENEMIES:

  • Basic: 100 PTS / 1 HP
  • Fast: 200 PTS / 1 HP
  • Power: 300 PTS / 1 HP
  • Armor: 400 PTS / 4 HP

BONUSES:

  • ⭐ Star · 🔫 Gun · ⛵ Boat · 💣 Grenade
  • ⏱️ Clock · 🛡️ Helmet · ⛏️ Shovel · ❤️ Tank
